What this simulation actually calculates

The animated inspiral offers nonspinning TaylorT4 radiation reaction through 3.5 post-Newtonian order, or the original leading-order model. The orbit is shown using the Newtonian frequency-to-separation relation; this is a coordinate proxy, not proper relativistic distance. Masses determine horizon radii; luminosity distance, redshift, and inclination determine the leading-order waveform amplitude.

Ω² = GM / r³
dr/dt = −64 G³ηM³ / (5c⁵r³)
fGW = Ω / π

These equations describe the leading-order option. Here M = m₁ + m₂, η = m₁m₂/M², and r is the separation proxy. TaylorT4 evolves v through dv/dt = 32ηv⁹B(v)/(5M), with the full nonspinning 3.5PN series B(v), using geometric mass-time M. Numerical quadrature and continuous interpolation make scrubbing independent of render rate. LVK: the basic physics of GW150914.

Scientific waveform workspace

TaylorF2 independently evaluates positive-frequency complex h+ and h× using the stationary-phase approximation, selectable 0–3.5PN phase, and restricted leading-order amplitude. It exports only inspiral bins and never includes the illustrative merger bridge. Source coefficients and conventions are pinned to LALSuite source, with derivations in Buonanno et al. (2009).

The self-check compares coefficients, integrated trajectories, and both complex polarizations with independent regression references. The waveform fixture is analytically transformed from a stored LALSuite tidal test vector; it is not a fresh native LALSuite run. Agreement tests implementation, not accuracy against a numerical-relativity merger.

Where approximation begins

The inspiral stops at r = 6GM/c², a Schwarzschild test-particle reference, not an exact merger boundary for two comparable masses. A continuous 30GM/c³ bridge illustrates coalescence. Its shape, duration, excitation amplitude, and visual motion are choices, not a numerical-relativity solution.

The remnant uses a catalog final-mass median when available, otherwise the nonspinning fit in Pan et al., Eq. 29. Remnant spin always uses that fit, calibrated at mass ratios 1–6. Larger ratios are labeled extrapolations.

h(t) ∝ e−t/τ cos(2πf220t + φ)
τ = Q / (πf220)

Ringdown uses the fundamental Kerr 220 mode from Berti, Cardoso & Will, Table VIII. It omits higher modes and overtones.

The important distinctions

  • This is not a solution of the full Einstein field equations, a precision merger template, or an event reconstruction.
  • Spin/precession, eccentricity, matter, tidal disruption, exact lensing, higher harmonics, and detector calibration are not modeled.
  • The scientist workspace includes an ideal right-angle detector response in local coordinates and explicitly synthetic white noise. This is not a real detector's sky response or measured observing-run sensitivity.
  • Source-frame masses set physical sizes. Observer times stretch by 1 + z; frequencies decrease by the same factor.
  • The h+ plot is modeled polarization, not observed detector strain. The measured GW150914 chart below is separate.
  • Contour light is false color. Black-hole mergers in vacuum do not require luminous disks.
  • Published uncertainty intervals remain in the catalog snapshot. Varying controls is not posterior sampling.

What “all data” means here

The complete raw LIGO detector archive, posterior samples, and every collaboration data product are not included or analyzed. The source indexes link to those resources. No finite publication list can establish coverage of unpublished work or every collaboration contribution.
